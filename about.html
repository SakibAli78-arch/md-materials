WHO WE ARE ?

MD Materials is a premier construction and building supplies partner based in Khargone, Madhya Pradesh, India. We specialize in sourcing and delivering industrial-grade building materials directly to construction sites, commercial developments, and residential builds.

Operating through our portal at www.mdmaterials.in, we bridge the gap between primary manufacturers, quarries, and on-site project execution. We eliminate supply bottlenecks by offering lab-tested, BIS-compliant materials backed by direct wholesale pricing and dedicated logistics.

What We Deliver

We provide an end-to-end supply chain covering every phase of your project from foundation to finish:


  • Civil Foundation & Structural Steel: High-ductility primary TMT rebars (Fe-550D) engineered for superior tensile strength, seismic resilience, and heavy framing.

  • High-Grade Cements & Binders: BIS-certified Ordinary Portland Cement (OPC 53/43), Portland Pozzolana Cement (PPC), structural concrete mixes, and premium wall care putties.

  • Masonry & Framing Supplies: Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC) lightweight blocks, high-density kiln-burned red clay bricks, and solid concrete masonry blocks

  • Aggregates & Ready-Mix: Clean-graded stone aggregates (10mm, 20mm, 40mm), washed river sand, manufactured sand (M-Sand), and coordinated Ready-Mix Concrete (RMC) dispatches.
